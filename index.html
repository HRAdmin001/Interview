Initial Video Interview

This is a recorded video interview. Our AI interviewer will greet you and ask a few questions out loud, one at a time. After each question you'll have a few seconds to think, then your answer is recorded on video.

Please read before you start:

  • Each question has its own time requirement. The suggested minimum and the maximum time are shown on screen for every question.
  • You can stop and save your answer at any time by clicking "Stop & save answer". If you don't, recording stops automatically when the time limit is reached.
  • There are no do-overs. Once you answer a question, your recording is final.
  • When time is up, the interview moves on. Recording stops automatically and the next question begins.
  • The whole interview takes about 15–20 minutes. Find a quiet, well-lit place, keep your face in view of the camera, and use headphones if you can.

Camera and microphone check

Make sure your face is clearly visible. Say a few words: the green bar should move when you speak. Turn your volume up so you can hear the interviewer.
